# AutoBot

Press the **Bot** button next to Play on a level page (or **Bot: off** in the pause menu) and the bot plays the level for you:

1. It learns the level behind a "learning" screen: sped up (30x by default) and muted, by trial and error. After each death it tries a new input a bit before the place it died.
2. Then it plays the level from the start in one attempt at 1x.
3. The route is saved, so next time the level is passed on the first attempt right away.

Classic levels only (no platformer or 2-player). Safe mode (on by default) keeps bot runs from saving progress, stars or leaderboard results.
